How to Play

  1. Click Play Game from the main menu.
  2. Read the fraction question shown on screen.
  3. Choose the correct answer from the options.
  4. Each correct answer earns 10 points.
  5. After 5 questions, the game ends and your score is saved.
  6. View your scores on the Score screen.

Tips

  • To add fractions, find a common denominator first.
  • Simplify fractions by dividing numerator and denominator by their GCD.
  • Practice makes perfect — play again to improve!
